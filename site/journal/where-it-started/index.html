Where it all started

Born in 1979, I grew up between tending the machines at my grandfathers’ industrial textile factory and running around the Science Museum of Barcelona.

This drawing is how I like to remember that girl: holding on tight to a soft toy, a wrench and a little truck, all at once. Cuddles and tools, curiosity and care. Not much has changed.

The factory taught me that machines can be understood, taken apart and put back together. The museum taught me that science is something you can touch. Decades later, those two places are still behind almost everything I do: building websites, curating maker events, learning digital fabrication and robotics, and sharing all of it with others.
