Cities Coalition for Digital Rights

Designing the home of a global network of cities that defend human rights online.

By Mariona N. Jones

In November 2018, Barcelona, Amsterdam and New York launched the Cities Coalition for Digital Rights: a network of cities committed to protecting and upholding human rights on the internet, at the local and the global level. Today more than 60 cities around the world are members.

Every member city signs a Declaration built on five principles: universal and equal access to the internet and digital literacy; privacy, data protection and security; transparency, accountability and non-discrimination in data, content and algorithms; participatory democracy, diversity and inclusion; and open, ethical digital service standards, so that cities can decide on their own technological infrastructure.

At SokoTech we designed and developed the coalition's website for Barcelona City Hall, together with the illustrator and designer edgaar, who also created its illustrations. The site is where the coalition presents its member cities and their stories, shares projects such as the Global Observatory of Urban AI, publishes news and events, and invites new cities to join. Fittingly for a digital rights project, its content is shared under a Creative Commons licence.
