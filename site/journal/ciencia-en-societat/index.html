Ciència en Societat

November 20, 2026 | 2 MIN READ

Mariona N. Jones
A new logo and website for a foundation that brings science closer to society.

Logo & website

The Fundació Ciència en Societat was born in 2005 to promote and stimulate scientific culture in society. From the very beginning it put secondary education first: schools as key places to share science, and a way to spark scientific vocations among young people.

The foundation also promotes STEAMConf, the international learning conference, which celebrated its 10th edition on 5–8 November 2025.

I designed the foundation's logo, a DNA double helix next to its name, and built its website. The site is trilingual (Catalan, Spanish and English), light and simple, and just like this one, it is deployed with Netlify.

Visit the Ciència en Societat website.
