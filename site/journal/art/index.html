A small sample of my art: silkscreen, textile printing, clay & drawing

By Mariona N. Jones

Before screens took over most of my days, I studied visual communication & art, and I still love making things by hand. These are a few pieces from over the years, across different techniques.

Pattern runs through a lot of it, probably from growing up around my grandfathers’ textile factory. In Vida, a single figure is mirrored and repeated across printed fabric panels until it becomes a rhythm, with the word “Vida” (life) woven into every tile.

Silkscreen lets you layer flat colours and found images. This print mixes a cat, a classic red car and tyre tracks, with paw prints walking the other way.

Clay is the most physical of all. This piece is a coin for an imaginary “Death Metal New World Currency”, with three views of a human skull and the motto “In blood we trust”, a playful nod to anatomy and to what we choose to value.

And drawing is where everything starts. Skulls come back again and again in my work; this one is in charcoal on a pastel background of blue, green and red.
