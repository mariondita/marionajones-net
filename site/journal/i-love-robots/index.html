I Love Robots

My love for robots goes way back in time

I was born in Barcelona, in Catalonia, a region historically known for its textile industry. Following the family tradition, I joined my father and my brother at the textile factory my grandfather had founded in 1947, during Franco’s dictatorship.

After two years tending the knitting machines and printing fabrics, I realised that working as a factory operator was rather repetitive and monotonous. Watching the machines run all day, I began to wonder whether robots could take over the repetitive tasks, leaving people free to do the creative, thoughtful work. I was fortunate to be able to go back to school, and earned a B.F.A. in Communication Design, with a minor in Art History, and an M.Sc. in Professional Media & Media Management.

In 2003, while studying for my B.F.A., I was hired by Evolution Multimedia, a multimedia and web development agency, where I learned to translate my graphic design skills to the web. I stayed for three years, and at the end of 2006 I left to start my own studio, SixOneEight, L.L.C.

As a web designer and developer, I’ve always aimed for user-friendly interfaces, with an emphasis on information architecture and standards-compliant code, so the web community could spend more time designing and building, and less time debugging.
