South of 64

Logo & WordPress website for a multimedia storytelling workshop (2009)

By Mariona N. Jones

South of 64 is a multimedia storytelling workshop from the School of Journalism at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. The name points to the part of Illinois that lies south of Interstate 64. Each year, students spend a weekend in a small town in the region, documenting its people and stories with photography, audio and video.

The first edition was in 2009, in Murphysboro. Around 35 students from the School of Journalism and the College of Mass Communication & Media Arts spent three days there, working side by side with visiting professionals as a kind of multimedia boot camp.

I designed the South of 64 logo and designed and developed the website on WordPress. The logo plays on the interstate shield, with “64” inside the sign and a handwritten “South of” leading into it, like a road trip. The website gathered everything the students made that weekend in one place, with sections for the stories, the documentary and the photo galleries, and big full-width images to let the photography shine.

Building it on WordPress meant the team could keep adding stories and galleries themselves, without needing me for every update, which mattered for a project that would come back year after year to new towns.
