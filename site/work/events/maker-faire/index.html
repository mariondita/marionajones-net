Maker Faire
Barcelona & Girona

I was the director and curator of Maker Faire Barcelona, produced by SokoTech together with Sónar+D and Fab Lab Barcelona. What started as a Mini Maker Faire grew into a big celebration of the maker movement: in 2017 and 2018 it filled the Pavelló Italià at Fira de Barcelona with more than 200 local and international makers, exhibitions, workshops and live demos. In 2020 I also directed and curated the first Maker Faire Girona, a fully online edition for the Universitat de Girona. Read El País on Maker Faire Barcelona 2019.
