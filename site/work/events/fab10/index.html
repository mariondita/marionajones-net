Fab10
Barcelona

Fab10 was the tenth international conference and annual meeting of the Fab Lab network, held in Barcelona in July 2014 under the theme “From Fab Labs to Fab Cities”. Organised by IAAC | Fab Lab Barcelona and Barcelona City Council, with MIT’s Center for Bits and Atoms and the Fab Foundation, it gathered people from more than 250 Fab Labs in over 40 countries. I was the curator and director of the Fab10 Festival, the weekend when the conference opened its doors to the local community and the general public.
