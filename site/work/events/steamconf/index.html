STEAMConf
Barcelona

I co-founded and directed (2015-2022) STEAMConf Barcelona, an international learning conference about STEAM education (science, technology, engineering, arts and maths) and project-based learning. Organised by SokoTech (2015-2022) and now by the Fundació Ciència en Societat, with partners such as the Catalan Government, Barcelona City Hall, Universities (UPC, UPF, et al.), it brings teachers, researchers, artists and makers together, often to share the most inspiring local and international projects through talks, hands-on workshops and local experiences. Read Ryan Jenkins’ write-up of STEAMConf 2018 on the Wonderful Idea Co. blog, and Cabaret Mechanical Theatre’s story of STEAMConf 2022, where Sarah Alexander gave the opening keynote.
