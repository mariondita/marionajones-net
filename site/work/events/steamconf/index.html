STEAMConf
Barcelona

I co-founded and direct STEAMConf Barcelona, an international learning conference about STEAM education (science, technology, engineering, arts and maths) and project-based learning. Organised by STEAM SokoTech for the Fundació Ciència en Societat, with partners such as the UPC and BIST, it brings teachers, researchers, artists and makers together, often at CosmoCaixa, to share the most inspiring local and international projects through talks, hands-on workshops and local experiences.
